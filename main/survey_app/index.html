01 / Sensor Fusion & Edge AI

See what's invisible. Measure what matters.

Our dual-stream architecture couples lightweight YOLOv8 edge vision with 40Hz vibration dynamics. By calculating lookahead arrival time timpact = tvision + d/v, RoadSense pairs visual pavement distress with real suspension jolts, filtering out phantom road paint and shadows.

01 // HARDWARE AGNOSTIC
Consumer Smartphones
Mount any smartphone to your dashboard or handlebars. The calibrated self-learning suspension baseline accounts for different vehicle types and stiffness.
02 // ASTM D6433 STANDARD
Official PCI Scorecards
Raw video and telemetry are converted into industry standard Pavement Condition Index (PCI 0-100) and International Roughness Index (IRI m/km) metrics.
03 // SPATIAL DBSCAN
Canonical Deduplication
Haversine DBSCAN clusters multiple passes over the same pothole across different survey rides into verified canonical hazards with multi-ride confidence scores.
